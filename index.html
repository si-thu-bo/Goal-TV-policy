Goal TV — Privacy Policy

Live football scores, fixtures, highlights and TV channels for Myanmar fans.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we never collect your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • We do collect anonymous usage statistics and an advertising identifier through Google (Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob) so we can understand which features are used and show ads.
  • We collect a push-notification token so we can deliver match alerts you allow.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • You can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the app, or by emailing the contact below to request deletion.

1. Who we are

Goal TV ("the app", "we", "us") is an independent sports-information application. This policy explains what happens to information about you when you install and use the app.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Usage and diagnostics — via Google Firebase Analytics

The app records anonymous events about how the app is used. This is aggregated behaviour data, not a profile of you as a person.

EventWhat it tells us
first_open, app_open, session countsInstall, launch and retention volumes
screen_view, tab_selectedWhich section (Live, Scores, Highlights, Channels, Settings) is used
match_openedWhich fixture and league were opened
stream_playedThat playback started, and which source
ad_eventAd format and whether it was shown or tapped
language_selectedWhether Burmese or English was chosen

Along with these events, Google automatically attaches standard device and app metadata — device model, manufacturer, operating-system version, app version, screen size, language, and an approximate region derived from your IP address. We do not receive your IP address ourselves.

2.2 Advertising — via Google AdMob

The app displays Google AdMob ads (banner, interstitial and app-open). To serve and measure ads, Google processes:

  • the Google Advertising ID on your device (this is why the app declares the AD_ID permission);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address, where permitted by your device settings;
  • device model, OS version and language;
  • ad impressions, taps and conversions.

Ad personalisation is managed by Google under its own policy. You can reset or delete the Advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads), which stops personalised ads without affecting the rest of the app.

2.3 Push notifications — via Firebase Cloud Messaging

When you allow the Android notification permission, FCM issues a device registration token. We use it only to deliver announcements and match-related messages to that device. The token is stored on Google's infrastructure and in our own service; it is not linked to your identity.

2.4 Information stored only on your device

These preferences never leave your phone — they are written to local app storage:

  • chosen language and light/dark/system appearance;
  • leagues you star or filter;
  • kick-off reminder settings and dismissed update prompts.

They are erased when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

2.5 What we never collect

No account, no sign-in, no name, email or phone number; no address book, camera, microphone, files, call log or precise GPS location; no payment or card data. The app requests only two permissions of its own: internet access and the notification permission.

3. Why we process it

  • Operate the service — fetching fixtures, scores, highlights and channel lists, and delivering kick-off reminders.
  • Improve the app — understanding which competitions, tabs and languages are used so development effort goes where players actually look.
  • Fund the service through advertising — AdMob revenue pays for the servers and data feeds. The app is free and has no subscription.
  • Keep you safe from bad states — a server-side switch lets us disable the app during an outage rather than showing broken data.

Our legal bases for processing EU/UK visitors' data are legitimate interest for service operation and analytics, and consent for personalised advertising, which Google obtains through its own ad SDK.

5. Retention

  • Analytics and advertising data: retained by Google under its own Firebase retention settings (typically 2–14 months rolling) rather than by us.
  • Push-notification token: kept while the app is installed; discarded when you uninstall or when you ask us to remove it.
  • On-device preferences: kept until you clear app storage or uninstall.
  • We hold no database of named users, so there is nothing that could be leaked about you as an individual.

6. Your choices, access and deletion

Because we never link collected data to your identity, the practical controls are these:

  • Stop analytics and ad tracking: uninstall the app. This ends all further collection immediately.
  • Turn off personalised ads: Android → Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or reset it.
  • Turn off notifications: the in-app notifications switch, or Android → Settings → Apps → Goal TV → Notifications. You can also revoke the notification permission entirely.
  • Request deletion: email uchashisu@gmail.com from any address and describe the device or app install. We will forward the request to Google's data-removal process and confirm back to you within 30 days.
  • Object or restrict processing (EEA/UK): the same email address handles these requests.

You can also reach Google directly at myactivity.google.com for data Google holds about you.

7. Security, and one honest limitation

All data the app sends to its own backend, to Firebase and to AdMob travels over TLS-encrypted HTTPS. We store no passwords and no personal records, so there is no user database to breach.

Where encryption is outside our control: some live TV channels listed in the Channels tab are delivered by third-party broadcasters over plain HTTP, because those providers do not offer an encrypted endpoint. When you choose to watch one of those channels, the video request and the address you are requesting it from are visible on the local network. This carries no personal data from our side — no identifier, name or token is included in those requests — but if you are on a shared or monitored network you may prefer to use the HTTPS sources, which are clearly available for most channels.

8. Children and age

Goal TV is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them. We do not knowingly collect personal data from a child under 13. The app is ad-supported, and we configure our ad requests as not directed at children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data through the app, email uchashisu@gmail.com and we will remove it.

9. Content and copyright notice

Goal TV publishes schedules, scores, statistics, short highlight links and links to third-party broadcasts. It does not host, upload or transmit match video itself; streams are played directly from the source that provides them. If you hold rights to material linked from this app and want it removed, contact us at the email above and we will disable the link while we review the request.

10. Changes to this policy

We update this page when the app's data handling changes, and the "Last updated" date at the top reflects the current version. Continued use after a change means acceptance of the revised policy. Substantive changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.
